# ⚛️ The Damaged Quantum Lab Adventure

### A Quantum Mystery Adventure for Ages 12–15

> **Don't tell the player the rule first. Let them discover it.**

**The Damaged Quantum Lab Adventure** is an educational exploration game that introduces players to the fascinating world of quantum computing through experimentation, puzzles, observation, and discovery.

Instead of presenting quantum concepts as traditional lessons followed by quizzes, the game places the player inside a damaged futuristic quantum laboratory and challenges them to experiment with qubits and quantum gates to repair the Quantum Core.

The player becomes a **Quantum Explorer** and discovers the rules of quantum mechanics through gameplay.

---

## 🎮 Game Concept

The game begins inside a damaged quantum laboratory where the central **Quantum Core** has stopped functioning.

The laboratory's AI tells the player that repairing the Quantum Core requires understanding quantum information.

Rather than explaining everything immediately, the game allows the player to experiment.
